A plan you can actually follow

Most planners show you everything at once. Dayfold shows you what to do next, and fits it into the time you have.

  • Deadline-aware ordering

    Dayfold looks at what is due and when, then puts the closest deadline first. Overdue work is flagged so it never hides at the bottom of a list.

  • Plans around your hours

    Say you have three hours after class. The plan fits into three hours, not ten.

  • Breaks built in

    Every study block ends with a 10-minute break, so the plan is one you can keep up.

  • One list for today

    Open the app and see only today. Everything else waits until tomorrow.

  • Weekly look-back

    On Sunday, see what you finished and what slipped, then carry it into next week.

  • Quiet reminders

    One nudge when a block starts. No streak guilt and no pile of notifications.

    Questions

    Is Dayfold a real app?

    No. Dayfold is a fictional product made for a frontend internship project at Progree. The planner on this page runs in your browser, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

    Does the demo use AI?

    The demo uses simple rules: nearer deadlines and higher priority go first, and tasks are packed into the hours you enter. The product pitch imagines an AI version that could also read a syllabus, but this page does not include one.

    What happens to tasks that do not fit today?

    They move to tomorrow. The plan shows them in a separate list so you can see what is waiting.

    Does it work on my phone?

    Yes. The page and the planner adjust to phones, tablets and laptops.
